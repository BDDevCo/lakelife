01 / Getting connected

Start with your
park office.

LakeLife gives you a place to view your own park account. Your office provides the instructions that connect you to the right lot and can help if you have trouble getting in.

1 / Ask for instructions

Ask the office for your LakeLife account invitation or claim instructions. Keep any claim code private; it connects you to your own record.

2 / Check your account

Review your bill breakdown, recorded payments and available receipts. If a charge or payment looks wrong, ask the office to review it with you.

3 / Use the stated payment method

Your account shows any online payment options enabled for your park. If online payments are unavailable, keep paying the office using its stated method.

Already paid the office?

Use “I’ve already paid” when it appears in your account to report a payment you made. The office reviews and confirms the record. Reporting a payment does not send money or mean the office has confirmed receipt.

Need help? Contact your park office using its usual phone number, office hours or contact method. Do not put your address, balance, payment details or claim code in a public social-media comment.

To learn about LakeLife, visit LakeLife.ai. For your park account and bill, begin with your park office.

02 / Work and optional care

Know where to ask.
See the next update.

Report a property issue

If your park has installed a LakeLife reporting sticker, follow the instructions on it. That reporting route does not require an app or login. If no sticker is available, ask the office to log the issue. You can follow reported-issue status and any recorded resolution in your account.

For an urgent hazard, use your park’s urgent contact procedure. An online report does not guarantee immediate attention.

Choose optional home care

Participating households can explore available care such as seasonal home service, boat care, lawn care or housekeeping. Review the scope, availability and pricing status. Some requests await a crew quote. Optional services are separate from your rent bill.

Understand who sees what

Your park office can see that a crew visited, what service was provided and when. It does not see what you paid for that optional service. Your account also provides your text-message preferences.

Three things to remember

Check your own account. Ask the office about a bill or reported issue. Review optional care and its pricing status before requesting work.

Getting started: ask your participating park office for your LakeLife account instructions. Keep them private and let the office know if the account shows the wrong lot.
