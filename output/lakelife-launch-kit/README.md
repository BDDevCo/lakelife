# LakeLife launch kit

Prepared 17 October 2026. Open `index.html` to browse the complete kit.

## Internal planning
- Business Plan: 10 pages. Includes internal service economics, clearly labeled assumptions, a quarterly first-year cash model, operating gates and decisions.
- Audience & Market Study: 8 pages. Primary-source desk research, audience hypotheses and an interview plan. No customer interviews were conducted.
- 90-Day Marketing Rollout: 8 pages. Proposed 19 October 2026–16 January 2027 schedule, $500 and $1,500 monthly budget options, funnel targets and outreach drafts.
- Social Media Playbook: 12 pages. Twelve captions, six graphics, two video scripts, replies and a working cadence.

## Customer-facing materials
- Park Owner Guide: 7 pages. Benefits, an illustrative bill, work coordination, resident value, setup, questions and walkthrough notes.
- Renter Welcome Guide: 3 pages. For participating parks; explains account access, payment reporting, issues and optional care.

PDFs live in `../pdf/`. Editable HTML versions sit here. The HTML uses the original artwork and fonts in `../lakelife-brand-guide/assets/`; keep that folder structure together. Browser print settings: Letter, background graphics on, no margins, headers/footers off.

## Working files
- `social/`: six 1080×1080 PNGs and editable HTML compositions using original LakeLife artwork.
- `social-calendar.csv`: dates, audience, captions, image filenames and alt text. Drafts only.
- `campaign-tracker.csv`: blank weekly record. No fabricated results.
- `prospect-research.csv`: public research examples, not verified customers or serviceable prospects.
- `business-model.csv`: illustrative model values; static editable inputs/outputs, not a formula-driven forecast.

The complete ZIP is an internal package because the business plan includes margin information. The separate recruitment ZIP contains only customer-facing PDFs. No posts were scheduled, no messages sent, no advertising purchased and no product behavior changed.

Sources are linked beside claims in the market study and identified in the business plan. Current repository workflows were reviewed without verifying production account configuration or making a live payment. No claim of universal live online payments, guaranteed savings or a fixed setup time is made.
