03 / Brand foundation

Calm enough to trust.
Clear enough to act.

LakeLife keeps the details of property care and park work understandable. The interface should answer: where am I, what is true, what needs me and what happens next?

Local and capable

Use familiar property language: your park, your lake home, crew, service, total, photos and office. Name Big Long, Pretty and Big Turkey Lakes when relevant.

Specific reassurance

Show the exact next step and its consequence. “Request received. Your date is not confirmed yet” is stronger than an unexplained success animation.

One main action

Give each task area a clear primary action. Keep supporting actions quiet and destructive actions separated from routine progress.

Truth before decoration

A missing read is not zero. A queued message is not delivered. A bill is not collected money. Completion requires its evidence.

Brand voice: “See the price before anything is charged.”
“Add the remaining photos to finish this job.”
“Ask your office to review this payment record.”

Keep the current approved public headline in its current context. The prototype’s homeowner tagline and the app’s newer public headline are not interchangeable instructions to overwrite page copy.

16 / Forms and verification

Make each field
feel answerable.

Labels

Keep a persistent label, optional helper and specific error. Placeholder text is an example, not the label. Use the existing .ll-field structure and styling.

Input behavior

Use the appropriate input type, autocomplete and inputMode. Preserve user input when a server action fails. Show errors beside the field and focus the first invalid field after submit.

Verification

Use the shared CodeBoxes component. It handles paste, backspace, autocomplete and the completed-code callback. Do not copy six independent inputs into a new form.

Before first booking

A working email and SMS-verified mobile are required. Explain the remaining step; never infer verification from a field merely containing a value.

Save behavior

One coherent form, one scoped save action. Show pending and saved states. If a second action has a separate consequence, label it as a separate task.

Recommended error: “Enter a mobile number we can text.”
Recommended recovery: “We couldn’t verify that code. Check the six digits or request a new code.”

Phone numbers may be stored in machine format but should display in the established readable format. Avoid technical provider errors in customer copy; keep diagnostic detail in authorized logs.

17 / Cards, tables and money

Keep the decision
beside the record.

PatternContract
Service cardService name, relevant scope, one customer total or truthful quote state, and a booking action. Do not show a $0 fallback for unavailable pricing.

18 / Dialogs and feedback

Never lose the task
behind a message.

MomentInterface contract
Dialog opensGive it a name, move focus inside, keep focus within the modal and provide a visible close control.
Dialog closesRestore focus to the trigger when appropriate. Escape and backdrop behavior must respect a write already in progress.
Waiting for a writePrevent repeated submit; keep “Saving…” tied to that action. Do not navigate away before showing a durable result.
Read failureExplain that the record could not be loaded and offer retry. Do not render fabricated empty rows or zero totals.
Write failurePreserve input, explain whether anything changed and provide a safe retry or a reconciliation step.

21 / Accessibility

Readable. Reachable.
Recoverable.

Review areaAcceptance target
Text contrastAt least 4.5:1 for normal text and 3:1 for large text. Measure actual rendered pairings.
Touch targetsLakeLife target: 44 px controls where practical. WCAG 2.2 AA target-size criterion uses 24 × 24 CSS px or permitted spacing/other exceptions.
KeyboardLogical order, visible focus, semantic controls and complete modal handling. Never require pointer-only interaction.
StructureOne descriptive h1; meaningful headings; associated labels; status text in addition to color.
ValidationSpecific errors with recovery. Announce changing results without forcing repeated screen-reader interruptions.

ImagesDescribe meaningful job evidence; decorative logo/waves can be hidden from assistive technology when the adjacent name conveys identity.
